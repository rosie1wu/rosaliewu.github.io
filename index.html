Things I believe:

  • First-hand experience first
    • The world is full of recycled information and inherited hierarchies. Most of what we think we know is secondhand
    • You can't outsource understanding—go see for yourself
  • Good solutions are hard to vary
    • They're specific to your constraints
    • If something applies everywhere, it explains little
    • Simplicity is derived from complexity
  • Take your work seriously, but not yourself
    • Obsess over the problem, with an open mind
    • Better to set extremely high standards and fail than aim low and settle
